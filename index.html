About

Engineer who owns the whole build

I'm a Senior Software Engineer specialized in PHP and Laravel, with a full-stack background. On client projects I usually work as the sole developer — owning architecture, development, deployment and hand-over.

I've built large modular Laravel systems: a 9-module hospital management system, a 7-module multi-tenant ERP (HR, payroll, accounting, inventory) and a multi-instructor e-learning platform with secure video, Stripe payments and commission splitting.

I also build Arabic-first (RTL) products, Sanctum-secured mobile APIs, and I'm expanding into Flutter to deliver mobile apps backed by my own APIs.
